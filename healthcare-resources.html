A note about your pathway

Requirements are personal.

Licensure, credential evaluation, work authorization and immigration requirements depend on your profession, state and individual situation. No recruiter can guarantee a license, visa, job offer or timeline. Ask for clear written information and verify official requirements with the appropriate authorities.

Before you proceed
Make sure you understand who your employer will be, what the written agreement says, which costs may apply, and which steps are your responsibility. Take time to review documents and seek qualified advice when needed.
