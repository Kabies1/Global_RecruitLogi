Contact and candidate information

Privacy notice.

This notice explains how Global Recruitment and Logistics LLC uses information submitted through its contact and candidate application forms.

Information collected

The contact form may collect your name, email address, inquiry type, organization and message. The candidate application may collect your legal name, contact details, work eligibility response, education, employment history, professional licenses and certifications, references, availability, CV text or file, and application declarations and consents. Reference details are personal information too; share them only with permission.

How it is used

We use contact inquiries to respond to your request and applications to review your qualifications, communicate with you about the role, coordinate recruiting, and carry out background or exclusion screening only when required and with the consent you provide. If you separately opt in on the application, we may keep it in our candidate pool and contact you about other opportunities for up to 12 months from your consent. You can ask us to withdraw that permission. An application does not guarantee an interview, offer, placement, license or immigration outcome.

Access and storage

Contact inquiries and application records are stored in the company’s Supabase database; uploaded CVs are stored in a private file bucket. Access is restricted to authorized recruiting administrators. CV download links are temporary. New inquiry notification emails contain an inquiry ID only. Application notifications contain limited metadata and do not include your CV or full application. Emails sent before this notice was updated may contain inquiry details and follow the company’s email-retention process.

Health information

Do not submit medical records, diagnoses, immunization records, government identification, passport or immigration documents through these forms. If a role requires health or immunization verification, the company will explain the appropriate process separately.

Retention and your choices

We keep contact inquiries and applications, including uploaded CVs, for up to 12 months after closure. A record with no recorded activity for six months may be closed; the 12-month period then begins. We record activity when an authorized administrator updates the status or records a recruiting review or contact. Candidate-pool consent expires 12 months after you give it; after it expires, we stop considering you for other opportunities. To be considered again, submit a new application and select the optional candidate-pool consent. If you are hired, we move information needed for your employment to the company’s employment records and apply the retention schedule for those records.

We may retain information longer when required by law or when it must be preserved for a complaint, investigation, or legal matter. A legal hold pauses scheduled deletion. When the applicable period ends, we delete the record and CV from active database and file-storage systems. Provider backups or service logs may expire on separate provider schedules. Company mailbox emails follow the company’s email-retention process.

To ask to access, correct, withdraw future-opportunity consent, or delete your inquiry or application information, contact admin1@glblogistics.org or mkabeireho@glblogistics.org. We will verify requests and explain if we must retain particular information for a legal obligation or matter.

This schedule is intended for the company’s current U.S.-focused recruiting workflow. Different laws, contracts or legal holds may require different periods. Company administrators should confirm the applicable requirements and provider retention schedules.
