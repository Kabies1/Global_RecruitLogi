Before You Apply

Prepare your information and questions before taking the next step in your healthcare career.

A practical starting point

Get prepared for your next opportunity.

Taking the next step in your healthcare career begins with being well prepared. Before applying for a position, we encourage you to gather the essential information and documents that will help us understand your qualifications, professional background, and career goals.

Preparing these materials in advance can help make the application and recruitment process more organized and efficient.

01 · Your experience

Prepare an Updated CV or Résumé

Ensure that your CV reflects your current qualifications, professional experience, employment history, key skills, and relevant achievements. Include your contact information and clearly outline your responsibilities in previous and current roles.

For healthcare professionals, highlight relevant clinical experience, areas of specialization, and the types of healthcare settings in which you have worked.

02 · Your background

Organize Your Education and Employment History

Prepare details of your educational background, professional training, previous employers, job titles, and employment dates. Where applicable, gather copies of certificates, diplomas, degrees, transcripts, and other supporting documents that may be required during recruitment.

Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and consistent across your application materials.

03 · Credentials

Gather Professional License and Registration Information

If you work in a regulated healthcare profession, prepare details of your current professional license or registration, including the issuing authority, license number where appropriate, and expiration date.

For international applicants, additional credential evaluation, licensing examinations, or registration with the relevant destination-country authority may be necessary. Requirements vary by profession, location, and position.

04 · References

Prepare Professional References

Identify suitable professional referees who can speak to your qualifications, work experience, professional conduct, and performance. Where appropriate, confirm their willingness to serve as references and ensure that their contact details are current.

References may be requested at different stages of the recruitment process.

05 · The opportunity

Understand the Position You Are Applying For

Take time to review the job description, required qualifications, responsibilities, work location, schedule, and other stated employment conditions. Consider how your experience and professional goals align with the opportunity.

If the position involves international relocation, also consider the practical implications of moving, including work authorization, accommodation, transportation, and family needs.

06 · Questions

Prepare Your Questions

Recruitment is a two-way process. We encourage you to prepare questions about the role, employer, work environment, compensation, licensing requirements, recruitment timeline, and relocation arrangements where applicable.

Asking questions helps you understand the opportunity and make informed decisions before proceeding.

07 · Final review

Review Your Application Before Submission

Before submitting your application, check that your contact information is correct, your CV is current, and all requested information is complete. Ensure that documents are legible and that the information provided is truthful and accurate.

Only submit sensitive personal documents through the appropriate, secure channels when requested.

Need More Information?

We understand that preparing for a new healthcare opportunity, particularly one involving international recruitment, can raise questions. Our team is available to help clarify application requirements and explain the next steps in the recruitment process.

Explore our healthcare resources for additional information and guidance as you prepare for your next career opportunity.

You are also welcome to contact our team if you need clarification before applying. We encourage you to prepare carefully, ask questions, and take the next step with confidence.
