Licenses, Registrations and Compliance

Our commitment to responsible, transparent recruitment practices.

Responsible Operations

Global Recruitment and Logistics is committed to operating responsibly, maintaining transparent recruitment practices and complying with applicable business, employment and recruitment requirements.

Our approach emphasizes professional conduct, fair recruitment, clear communication and respect for candidates and employers throughout the recruitment process.

We continually review the requirements relevant to our recruitment and workforce services and work with appropriate professionals and authorities when specialized guidance is needed.

Transparency matters. Information about applicable business registrations, licenses and professional credentials will be provided where relevant and verified.

Note: This statement describes our commitment to compliance. It does not represent a claim that any particular license or accreditation has been obtained.
