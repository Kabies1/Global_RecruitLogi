Relocation Planning for International New Hires

We understand that you may have important questions about your relocation journey. Below are some sample steps and practical considerations to help you gain a glimpse of what to expect as you prepare to move to a new country for employment.

Our goal is to make your transition as smooth and comfortable as possible by offering practical support as you settle into your new environment. Please feel free to contact our team at any time if you have questions or need further clarification. We are here to guide you throughout your relocation journey.

01 · Prepare

Immigration and Travel Preparation

Ensure that you understand the applicable visa requirements, work authorization, travel documentation, and other preparations necessary before departure. Our team will provide guidance and help you understand the steps involved in preparing for your move.

02 · Housing

Housing and Accommodation

We arrange accommodation and offer the first month of accommodation free of charge to help you settle in comfortably as you adjust to your new environment. During this period, you can familiarize yourself with the local area and explore longer-term housing options that suit your needs and budget.

03 · Arrival

Travel and Arrival Arrangements

We arrange airport pickup to welcome you upon arrival and help you get to your accommodation. We also provide initial transportation support as you settle in and organize your own ongoing transportation arrangements for commuting to work and meeting your daily needs.

04 · Budget

Financial Preparation

Prepare a realistic budget for your initial expenses, including food, transportation, phone services, personal necessities, and other living costs. Our team can help you understand the expenses you may need to plan for during your first few weeks.

05 · Family

Family and Dependents

If you are relocating with family members, consider their documentation, schooling, childcare, healthcare access, and other settling-in needs. Our team can offer general guidance to help you plan ahead.

06 · Orientation

Orientation and Settling In

We provide practical support to help you become familiar with your new surroundings, including local customs, community resources, public transportation, essential services, and workplace expectations.

07 · Employment

Employment Onboarding

Stay in contact with your recruitment team and employer regarding your start date, onboarding requirements, workplace orientation, and any outstanding documentation.

08 · Ongoing support

Ongoing Settling-In Support

Our support extends beyond your arrival. We aim to help you settle in, understand your immediate surroundings, and navigate the initial transition into your new life and employment. If you have questions or encounter challenges during this period, please contact our team for guidance.

Our Commitment to You

Relocating internationally is a significant step, and we understand that having the right support makes a difference. From arranging your initial accommodation and airport pickup to providing practical assistance as you adjust to your new environment, our goal is to help you settle in with greater confidence and peace of mind.

Please reach out to our team whenever you need clarification or assistance. We look forward to supporting you as you begin this exciting new chapter.

Specific relocation arrangements and support will be communicated to you before your departure so that you understand what to expect and can plan accordingly.
